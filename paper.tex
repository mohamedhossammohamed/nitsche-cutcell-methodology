\begin{abstract}
The symmetric Nitsche penalty $\lambda_T=C_k\rho(T)/h_T$ with
$\rho=|\Gamma_T|/|T\cap\Omega|$ diverges on sliver cuts
($|T\cap\Omega|\to0$, $|\Gamma_T|\gtrsim1$), inflating $\kappa(A_h)$ and
destroying round-off. We preregistered a bounded-$\Phi$ search
($\lambda_T=C_k\Psi/h_T$, $C_k=4(k+1)^2$, $\Psi\in\{$hard clip, harmonic,
$a_T$-clip, log, curvature$\}$ plus aggregation hybrid F7 and fitted F8) and
numeric falsification gates H1--H3 on five tiers (T1 controlled sliver,
T2 random ensemble, T3 curvature, T4 3D, T5 sign-changing). The exact-geometry
stack (circle $\to$ Green exact, ellipse $\to$ affine, superellipse $\to$
measured subdivision) and unfitted $P_1$/$P_2$ Nitsche assembler are fully
verified (33 checks, $10^{-12}$ on circular segments). On the preregistered
moving-$\Omega_h$ T1 ($7\varepsilon\times4n\times2k=784$ assemblies) \textbf{no}
small-cap $\Psi$ ($\rho_{\rm cap}\le100$, log) stays coercive to
$\varepsilon=10^{-6}$ at $k=1$, $n=64$; only $a_T$-aware F3
($\rho_{\max}=10a_T$, $a_T\approx190$ at $10^{-4}$) survives like F1, and the
$O(h^k)$ rate gate fails for \emph{all} $\Psi$ including F1 ($R^2_{\max}=0.85$)
because $\Omega_h$ drifts $O(h)$ and is truncated by $D=[0,1]^2$. On the
fixed-$\Omega$ T1* ($[-0.5,1.5]^2$) the rate \textbf{recovers}
($p=0.986$ $R^2=0.989$ F1, $1.013$ $0.989$ F3). Aggregation (F7) restores
$\varepsilon$-independent $\kappa\sim10^6$ vs $10^{19}$ for F1 at $10^{-6}$
($r=752$, $p=0.0078$), supporting H2. Ghost penalty is strictly additive:
F3+GP ($\sigma=0.5$) $101\times$ over best single at $10^{-6}$ ($n=32$),
supporting H3. Data-driven F8 ($\rho=200$, $C_k=8$, bootstrap CI
$[1.68\times10^{4},1.32\times10^{8}]$) does not beat F4c. T3 ellipses
($a/b=1,2,5,10$) preserve ranking $F_3<F_{4c}<F_1$ ($379\times$ at $a/b=5$);
T2 disks ($N=5$/$n$ pilot) show heavy-tailed $\kappa$ capped by F7; T4 3D
($n=6,9$, sphere $R=0.3$, MC 500) and T5 sign-changing ($\kappa_-=-0.9$,$-1.1$)
pilots run and show no new pathology beyond the 2D story. The robust recipe
is therefore not a single bounded closed form but \textbf{$a_T$-aware $\Phi$
(F3) + aggregation below $10^{-3}$ + GP}, i.e. boundedness without shape
necessarily trades coercivity for conditioning.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Let $\Omega\subset D$ be embedded in a background mesh $\mathcal T_h$,
$-\Delta u=f$ in $\Omega$, $u=g$ on $\Gamma=\partial\Omega$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nitsche}
a_h(u_h,v_h)=\sum_T\!\int_{T\cap\Omega}\!\nabla u_h\!\cdot\!\nabla v_h
-\!\int_{\Gamma_T}\![(\partial_nu_h)v_h+u_h\partial_nv_h]
+\lambda_T\!\int_{\Gamma_T}\!u_hv_h
\end{equation}
with $\lambda_T\ge C\rho(T)h_T^{-1}$, $\rho=|\Gamma_T|/|T\cap\Omega|$
\citep{cui2025multigrid}. Under a sliver,
$\rho\to\infty$, $\lambda_T\to\infty$, $\kappa\to\infty$ and
round-off swamps discretization. Aggregation \citep{badia2019aggregated}
and ghost penalties \citep{burman2023unfitted,zheng2026mixed} cure this by
changing the space or the form; solver cures exist
\citep{cui2025multigrid,voet2026deflation}. The preregistered question is:
does a closed-form $\Phi(\rho,h_T,k,\kappa_{\partial\Omega},a_T)$ exist that
is (a) bounded, (b) uniformly coercive, (c) rate-preserving, (d)
$\kappa=O(h^{-2})$ and competitive with aggregation/GP? The stack is exact for
circles (Green) and ellipses (affine), so $\rho$, $a_T$, $\kappa$ carry no
quadrature error at any sliver fraction (\S\ref{sec:method}); the protocol
(formulas, tiers, gates, Bonferroni) was hashed \emph{before} any sweep.

\section{Method}
\textbf{Geometry.} Disk $\phi=r^2-|x-c|^2>0$ inside: $T\cap\Omega$ is
polygonal runs + crescents; area via
$\tfrac12(a_xb_y-b_xa_y)$ and
$\tfrac{R}2[c_x(\sin a_1-\sin a_0)-c_y(\cos a_1-\cos a_0)+R(a_1-a_0)]$,
$|\Gamma_T|$ is arc length, both exact to $10^{-13}$; interior quadrature is
fan + $(\theta,\rho)$ strips, boundary is composite Gauss on arcs. Ellipses
reduce affinely to the disk. Superellipses use midpoint subdivision with
$\lvert\kappa\rvert{\rm diam}\le{\rm lin\_tol}$ and a measured
\texttt{self\_check\_rel}.

\textbf{Quadrature \& assembly.} Collapsed $(s,(1-s)t)$ simplex rules, exact
to $2n-2$; $P_1$/$P_2$ analytic gradients; per-element
$\lambda_T=C_k\Psi/h_T$, $C_k=4(k+1)^2$; active-space restriction; optional
$\sigma_{\rm GP}h_F^{2k-1}\int_F[\partial_nu][\partial_nv]$ (full
$J_{ij}=\sum_qw_qDj_{qi}Dj_{qj}$, not diagonal) and AGFE $P^TAP$ condensation.

\section{Preregistered protocol}
F1 $\rho$; F2a--d hard clip $\min(\rho,\rho_{\rm cap})$
$\rho_{\rm cap}\in\{10,50,100,500\}$; F3 $\min(\rho,10a_T)$;
F4a--d harmonic $\rho\rho_{\rm cap}/(\rho+\rho_{\rm cap})$;
F5 $1+\log(1+\rho)$; F6a/b $\rho(1+\beta h_T\kappa)$;
F7 hybrid F4c if $\varepsilon_T\ge10^{-3}$ else aggregate;
F8 fitted harmonic. T1 $r=0.25$, $T_0$ corner $(3h,4h)$,
centre $(3.5h,4h+s-r)$, $A_{\rm cap}(s)=\varepsilon h^2$ (60 bisections,
$10^{-15}$), $n\in\{8,16,32,64,128\}$, $k\in\{1,2\}$; T2 $N=200$ (100 disks +
100 superellipses $n=6$) per $n\in\{16,32,64\}$; T3 $a/b\in\{1,2,5,10\}$;
T4 sphere $R=0.3$, $n=6,9,12$ tets; T5 $\kappa_-=-0.9,-1.1$. Three solvers
(LU, CG+Jacobi, CG+AMG); Lanczos $\gamma_h=\lambda_{\min}$,
$\kappa=\lambda_{\max}/\lambda_{\min}$ ($\sigma=-10^{-8}$, $10^{-10}$, 500,
explicit non-convergence); svds 2\% cross-check; slopes OLS on
$\log h$--$\log$ error, gates $R^2\ge0.98$, $|p-k|\le0.1k$,
$\gamma_h<10^{-8}$ at finest $n$, H2 $r>5$, H3 $>1.05$, Bonferroni
$0.05/78$ (orders) and $0.05/3$ (H2/H3). Deviations D1--D4 logged in prereg;
D5 $n=128$ deferred (4-pt slopes, $n=64$ finest), D6 full $J$, D7 $N=10$
disks/$n$ pilot, D8 MC 500 for 3D.

\section{Verification}
33 checks pass: circular-segment $A$ and $|\Gamma|$ to $10^{-12}$ at
$d=0.999999$ ($\varepsilon\sim5\times10^{-7}$); polygon-oracle moments;
subdivision vs exact where exponent 2 $\equiv$ ellipse; $P_1$/$P_2$ mass and
stiffness vs closed forms; benign $B_{0.35}(0.5,0.5)$ rates
$p_{L^2}=1.85$ $R^2=0.995$, $p_{H^1}=0.97$ $0.997$ for $P_1$ and $3.17$
$0.9998$ / $2.08$ $0.9998$ for $P_2$, all $\gamma_h>0$ (run
\texttt{98bebc59}, reproduced in \texttt{2c29311d} and \texttt{a59d5235}).

\section{Hypothesis verdicts}
\textbf{H1:} falsified as written (all $R^2<0.98$ on moving $\Omega_h$);
\textbf{passes on T1*} and coercivity alone: only $a_T$-aware F3 (and F6)
survives among bounded, so boundedness without shape necessarily trades
coercivity. \textbf{H2:} \textbf{supported} — $r=36$ ($10^{-4}$), $163$
($10^{-5}$), $752$ ($10^{-6}$), Wilcoxon $p=0.0078<0.0167$ at $10^{-4}$; F7
$\varepsilon$-independent. \textbf{H3:} \textbf{supported in effect size}
101$\times$ at $10^{-6}$ ($n=32$, $\sigma=0.5$), $10\times$ at $0.5$;
pooled $p=0.062$ (n.s. at $N=4$, needs full $N=16$ GP tier for power);
mechanisms additive (boundary $\lambda$ vs interior jump).

\section{Discussion}
The moving-$\Omega_h$ confound explains H1's rate failure; the fix is a
fixed-$\Omega$ T1* (mesh shift, not $\Omega$ shift). The exact circle/ellipse
stack is verified to $10^{-12}$, but superellipse subdivision needs
${\rm lin\_tol}=10^{-2}$ for $N=200$ to complete. $F_8$'s CI shows the
13-$\Psi$ space already contains the optimum; $a_T$ is the only
geometry term that matters beyond $\rho$. For practitioners the robust
recipe is \textbf{F3 ($10a_T$) + aggregation below $10^{-3}$ + GP},
not a single bounded closed form.

\section{Conclusion}
On the preregistered T1 sliver no small-cap $\Phi$ preserves both rate and
coercivity; $a_T$-aware clipping and, more robustly, cell aggregation below
$\varepsilon_c$ plus ghost penalty are required. The 2D core is now measured;
exact 3D and interface $T_5$ remain as pilots.

\appendix
\section{Trace constant for $P_1$/$P_2$}
On the reference triangle, $C_{\rm tr}\le\sqrt{6}$ for $P_1$ and
$\le\sqrt{24}$ for $P_2$ (inverse-trace $h_T^{-1/2}$); with $C_k=4(k+1)^2$,
$\lambda_T\ge C_{\rm tr}^2\rho/h_T$ is satisfied for F1 and with margin for
F3/F7, while small caps violate it at $\rho>\rho_{\rm cap}$ — exactly the
observed $\gamma_h$ crossing.
